GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Honest answers about ordering from GiftWALA. For the full details, see our Delivery & Returns, Terms and Privacy pages.

How much is delivery?

Delivery charges are Rs. 300 per order. An empty bag has no delivery charge.

How long will my order take to arrive?

Personalised gifts are made to order, so the delivery time depends on the item. We confirm the expected delivery time with you on WhatsApp when you place your order, before you pay anything.

How can I pay?

Cash on Delivery is available. Prepaid orders through Easypaisa, JazzCash or Bank Transfer get 15% off. Payment details are shared with you at checkout — only there, so you always see them in the context of your own order.

Can I return a personalised gift?

Because personalised gifts are made specially for you, they cannot be returned for a change of mind. If your order arrives damaged, defective, or is the wrong item, contact us on WhatsApp as soon as you receive it, with your order ID and a photo, and we will arrange a replacement or refund. See Delivery & Returns.

How do I personalise a gift?

On the product page you can add the name exactly as you want it printed, a gift note, and an occasion card. For photo gifts, choose a photo — the file itself is not uploaded to our server; we confirm it with you on WhatsApp. Please check spelling carefully before confirming, because we make the item exactly as you write it.

How do I track my order?

You receive an order ID after checkout. Enter it on the Track Order page to see the latest delivery status.

Do you deliver across Pakistan?

Yes, we deliver nationwide across Pakistan.

Can I personalise a gift with a name or photo?

Yes — many gifts can be personalised with a name, date, photo or short message. Choose a gift, add your details on its page, and we confirm everything with you on WhatsApp before your order is made.
