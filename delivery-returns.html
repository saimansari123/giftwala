Delivery & returns.

Everything you need to know before you order from GiftWALA.

Delivery across PakistanWe deliver nationwide. Delivery charges are Rs. 300 per order. Delivery charges are only added when your bag has items in it — an empty bag has no delivery charge.
Delivery timePersonalised gifts are made to order, so the delivery time depends on the item. We confirm the expected delivery time with you on WhatsApp when you place your order, before you pay anything.
PaymentCash on Delivery is available. Prepaid orders through Easypaisa, JazzCash or Bank Transfer get 15% off. Payment details are shared with you during checkout.
Returns & exchangeBecause personalised gifts are made specially for you, they cannot be returned for a change of mind. If your order arrives damaged, defective, or is the wrong item, contact us on WhatsApp as soon as you receive it, with your order ID and a photo, and we will arrange a replacement or refund.
